Idle / Active are per video source, in kbps. Ceiling = active × sources. Log + bytes means the ladder was read from that model's own VIDEO_AGENT_LOG and cross-checked against real byte data; Bytes only means byte data exists but no log pull. Every model below is measured — none are extrapolated.

Provenance. Encoder ladders come from VIDEO_AGENT_LOG dash-quality transitions on one online unit of each of 17 models, roughly 21 hours per model. Activity factors and peak ratios come from a 7-day hourly bandwidth export covering 41 cameras and 7 DR40 readers; 40 cameras contributed to the activity bands. Fifteen devices appear in both datasets; on those, the log-derived ceiling predicted actual bytes within ±17% wherever the scene was active.

Limits. All measurements come from one organisation, so per-model sample sizes are small (1–5 cameras). AI feature overhead is not separately validated. Activity dominates the error budget — two identical cameras in one building differed 500× on scene content alone. Recommended speed excludes non-Rhombus traffic sharing the uplink.
