Result

Provenance. Encoder ladders from VIDEO_AGENT_LOG dash-quality transitions, one online unit per model across 17 models, ~21 h each. Activity factors and peak ratios from a 7-day hourly byte export covering 41 cameras and 7 DR40 readers. Fifteen devices appear in both datasets; on those the log-derived ceiling predicted actual bytes within ±17% wherever the scene was active.

Limits. One organisation, so per-model samples are small (1–5 cameras). AI feature overhead is not separately validated: the export reports one total per device, so per-feature cost cannot be split from scene activity. Activity dominates the error budget — two identical cameras in one building differed 500× on scene content alone, so rate each camera on its own view. Recommended speed excludes non-Rhombus traffic on the uplink.
