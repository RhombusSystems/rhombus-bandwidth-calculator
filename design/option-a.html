Where these numbers come from

Encoder ladders were read from VIDEO_AGENT_LOG dash-quality transitions on one online unit of each of 17 models, roughly 21 hours per model. Activity factors and peak ratios come from a 7-day hourly bandwidth export covering 41 cameras and 7 DR40 readers. Fifteen devices appear in both datasets; on those, the log-derived ceiling predicted actual bytes within ±17% wherever the scene was active.

Known limits

All measurements come from one organisation, so per-model sample sizes are small (1–5 cameras). AI feature overhead is not separately validated — the export gives one total per device, so per-feature cost cannot be split from scene activity. Activity is the largest source of error: two identical cameras in the same building differed by 500× purely on scene content, so rate each camera on its own view rather than applying one profile site-wide. Recommended speed excludes non-Rhombus traffic sharing the uplink.
